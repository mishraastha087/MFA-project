Security Tips

Stay safe online

A few consistent habits go a long way in protecting your accounts. Here are five practices worth building into your routine.

1

Use strong passwords

Create long, unique passwords that mix letters, numbers, and symbols for every account.

2

Enable MFA

Turn on multi-factor authentication wherever it's offered to add a critical second layer of defense.

3

Never share your OTP

A one-time password is meant only for you — no legitimate service will ever ask you to share it.

4

Update passwords regularly

Refresh your passwords periodically, especially after any report of a data breach.

5

Beware of phishing attacks

Be cautious of unexpected emails or messages asking you to click links or enter your credentials. Always verify the sender before responding.
