What is MFA?

A quick introduction

Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is a login method that asks for two or more separate proofs of identity before granting access — instead of relying on a password alone. Each proof comes from a different category, so even if one factor is compromised, your account stays protected.

Something You Know

A password, PIN, or answer to a security question that only you should know.

Something You Have

A physical or digital item like your phone, an OTP code, or a security key.

Something You Are

A biometric trait such as your fingerprint, face, or voice.
